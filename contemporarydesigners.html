Contemporary Designers

So far, the history of the National Park and its design trends have been covered. What happens post Unigrid? The NPS still utilizes the unigrid for their designs. This section is not about covering NPS design standards but rather highlighting some contemporary artists (post 1980s), that make designs/art featuring the National Parks. Now that people have come to love and appreciate the National Parks, and a sense of experimentation and visual identity has already been discovered, let's look at some people who have taken the designs of the past and made something new.

See America

In 2016, a book came out called, “See America,” put together by Creative Action Network and National Parks Conservation Association. In order to commemorate the centennial of Yellowstone being founded, they issued a call to artists and designers worldwide to create posters of their favorite parks. Below I have taken some excerpts from the article, “See National Parks though Artists eyes”, by Nicolas Brulliard, highlighting some of the book’s artists.
