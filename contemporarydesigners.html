Contemporary Designers

So far, the history of the National Park and its design trends have been covered. What happens post Unigrid? The NPS still utilizes the unigrid for their designs. This section is not about covering NPS design standards but rather highlighting some contemporary artists (post 1980s), that make designs/art featuring the National Parks. Now that people have come to love and appreciate the National Parks, and a sense of experimentation and visual identity has already been discovered, let's look at some people who have taken the designs of the past and made something new.

See America

In 2016, a book came out called, “See America,” put together by Creative Action Network and National Parks Conservation Association. In order to commemorate the centennial of Yellowstone being founded, they issued a call to artists and designers worldwide to create posters of their favorite parks. Below I have written briefly about some of the artists from the article, “See National Parks though Artists eyes”, by Nicolas Brulliard. Check out National Parks Conservation Association: See National Parks through Artists eyes for more information on the pieces below!

The Art of the National Parks

In 2021, a book came out called, “The Art of the National Parks,” put together by Weldon Owen, Theresa Pierno, JP Boneyard, and Fifty-Nine Parks. This book was put together to feature contemporary national park posters, designed in order to pay homage to their WPA ancestors! Caroline Goldstein wrote an article called, “A New Book Puts a Contemporary Twist on the Beloved WPA-Era Posters that Promoted America’s National Parks - See Images Here,” that highlights some of these works. Check out Artnet: The Art of the National Parks for more information!

Outdoor Painter

In 2021, the Editor-In-Cheif of PlenAir Magazine, Kelly Kane, wrote an article called, “National Parks Through the Eyes of 6 Artists.” The 6 artists featured are all landscape painters, and their works resemble those of the American Landscape painters of out past. Check out Outdoor Painter: National Parks Through the Eyes of 6 Artists to learn more about the artist and their works!
